## 5. Ghost-leaflet (volume penalization) track — v22_penal.mph

Method: blood mesh FIXED; leaflets = meshed fluid domains with penalty force F = -(mu/K)*chi*(u - u_s),
u_s = omega x r; chi(x,t) via General Extrusion back-rotation onto the CAD leaflet (genA/genB);
torque TzA_pen = intAll(eta*chiA*((x+pin_x)(v-usAy)-(y-pin_y)(u-usAx))), etc.

## 6. Decided direction (Nov 9–10)
Option 1 chosen: hybrid ghost leaflet (bulk flow) + Reynolds thin-film hinge (50 um gap as 2D film on pocket
walls, h(x,t) = d0(R^-1 x) from CAD). Validity: eps = h/L ~ 0.025; eps*Re ~ 0.03 systole, ~0.24 closure.
Hinge WSS: tau_w = -/+ (h/2) dp/ds + mu*U_s/h (~270 Pa scale at closure).

Debate verdict (Advocate vs Challenger vs Judge) — phased plan with go/no-go gates:
| Phase | Work | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| A | Exact ghost volume -> fixed-angle validation at 70/75/80/84 deg; Bland–Altman, regression, paired t + FDR | bias < 5%, R2 > 0.95 |
| B | K study (1e-9, 3e-10, 1e-10) + 3-mesh GCI on ghost AND body-fitted reference | GCI < 5% |
| C | Thin film standalone, one-way, uniform-gap region only; mouths/corners kept in 3D; vs 3D real-hinge flux/WSS | within 10% |
| D | Prescribed opening, SMOOTHED volume-calibrated chi for motion (sharp chi only for fixed-angle validation) + film; block 3D pocket to avoid double counting | mass balance < 1%, smooth torque |
| E | Two-way FSI with added-mass stabilization (artificial inertia/damping sensitivity) | stabilization effect < 2% |
Fallback at every stage: two-scale result (blades FSI + real hinge fixed/prescribed). Thesis safe after A+B
plus existing fixed-angle results; C–E add novelty.
Key risks: film invalid at mouths/corners/closure; sharp moving chi -> force noise; extrusion gives no
d(chi)/d(theta) Jacobian -> FSI convergence; reference not mesh-converged; timeline.

Other options considered: optimal pivot fit (cheap, explains inverted elements; may prove rotation+sliding);
overset mesh (deferred: no COMSOL built-in, still needs overlap inside 50 um gap, ~2x DOF) -> PhD/follow-up.
Fluent Student: closed (11.5 M surface triangles -> 50–100 M cells vs ~1 M student limit; COE Apps Workbench has no Fluent).
